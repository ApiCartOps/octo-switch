---
description: Triage open issues in a repo (usage /gh-triage owner/repo)
argument-hint: owner/repo
---
Triage issues in $ARGUMENTS using the octoswitch MCP server. Call `gh_list_issues`, then `gh_get_issue` for the ones that look unclear. Group them as bugs, features and questions, flag duplicates, and suggest an order of work. Do not comment on or change anything.
